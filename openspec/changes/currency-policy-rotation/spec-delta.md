# Specification delta

## Added: Currency policies

The provider budget may append a new immutable policy only as a linear successor to the active policy. It must preserve complete historical proposal reservations and costs, use a shared account identity and non-overlapping UTC calendar-month pricing windows, and retain a unique exact price card identity on every proposal. A successor may not increase run/day/month allocations or decrease configured day/month baselines. Run/day/month caps aggregate settled usage and maximum uncertain reservations from every policy version within the matching UTC period; prior UTC periods remain retained but do not count in a later period. An open/uncertain reservation, unknown history, branch/fork, unpriced proposal or stale active pointer rejects policy rotation and provider dispatch. Prior policies and receipts remain verifiable after rotation. A call admitted and dispatched inside its pinned price window may settle afterward, including after midnight/expiry; it is costed with that exact reserved card and charged to the reservation's original UTC day/month. Unknown or excess usage remains uncertain and retains its maximum reservation.
