# Proposed specification delta

- Article revision context distinguishes source-check date from the latest actual Markdown source-change date. The latter is derived from complete source Git lineage pinned to the build revision, with commit and change summary; unavailable or shallow lineage and an explicitly absent source pin are shown as unknown.
- Readers can distinguish source consultation and prior technical/source review from human editorial certification. Review remains pending unless a real article review record exists. The twenty drafts received deep reading across external and internal review, while factual claim verification was sampled; no blanket factual certification is implied.
- Publication, maintenance, restoration, and correction receipts remain attributable to their recorded event and do not become article change dates or factual approval claims.
- Article pages expose useful page-specific sharing previews and copy-link/citation actions. Visible citation text remains available without JavaScript or when clipboard access fails.
- Explicit production builds provide a canonical, indexable History route and page-specific metadata. Staging and synthetic builds keep History noindex with no canonical URL.
- About describes the checks actually performed, their limits, and the undecided future review cadence. An empty maintenance-history view does not imply no article source changes.
- Preserve existing article text, reading identity, history compatibility, static stack, and release gates.
