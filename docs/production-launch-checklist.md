## Recovery

Keep the accepted source branches and Git ancestry, source bundle, exact qualification logs, immutable database/audit records, known-good staged archive and version/deployment receipts. Preserve previous failed connector uploads and the no-version journal as historical evidence. Do not reset a branch or erase an uncertain provider/write reservation to make a retry proceed. Where a known quality rejection can be terminalized, retain its charged proposal and immutable denial authority.

A change that creates new versioned authority records requires a compatible reader. Do not rewrite canonical history to make an older binary appear compatible. A crash-retained integration lock requires deliberate owner recovery; an ordinary retry is not proof that a previous operation failed.
