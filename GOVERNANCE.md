# Governance

The founder owns product direction, licensing, budgets, monetization, and external-account decisions. Authorized maintainers review changes. The founder's long-term goal includes autopilot publishing and maintenance. Human editorial publication review is the proposed initial posture, not a permanent founder requirement. Future bounded autopublishing requires an explicitly approved policy, measured factual/source checks, reversible revisions, and escalation rules. Automation does not establish truth through model confidence alone. Code review and deployment authorization are separate from editorial publication policy.

## Editorial standards
- Prefer original documentation, research, standards, and reproducible examples. Distinguish facts, vendor claims, analysis, uncertainty, and dated behavior.
- Cite claim-level evidence; record publication/version and last verification dates. Link to sources rather than reproducing substantial copyrighted text.
- Avoid promotional ranking, invented citations, unsupported superlatives, or manufactured freshness.
- Document conflicts of interest and sponsorship. Ads must not influence editorial conclusions.
- Correct substantive mistakes with a visible revision note; preserve Git history and record review identity.
- Initially propose maintainer editorial review for publication and consequential corrections; reassess it against the approved bounded-autopublishing policy. Policy changes require owner approval. These are proposed processes, not configured GitHub protection rules.

Before public contributions: select separate code/content license treatment as appropriate, publish submission terms and attribution rules, choose moderation and private security contacts, and establish retention/deletion policy.
